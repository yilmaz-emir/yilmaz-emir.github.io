October 6, 2026 — Paper accepted in Ceramics International

Our collaborative paper, “Laser Surface Texturing of Plasma-Sprayed TiO₂/ZnO Composite Coatings: Correlation between Microstructure, Wear Mechanisms, and Biological Response,” has been accepted for publication in Ceramics International.

The study examines the relationships between laser surface texturing, coating microstructure, wear mechanisms, and biological response. Congratulations to all co-authors, and thank you for your contributions to this collaborative work.

DOI: 10.1016/j.ceramint.2026.10.026
