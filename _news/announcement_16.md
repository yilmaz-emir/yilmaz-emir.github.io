Our collaborative work titled **“Laser Surface Texturing of Plasma-Sprayed TiO₂/ZnO Composite Coatings: Correlation between Microstructure, Wear Mechanisms, and Biological Response”** has been accepted for publication in *Ceramics International*.

The study examines the relationships between laser surface texturing, coating microstructure, wear mechanisms, and biological response.
