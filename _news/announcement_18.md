We are pleased to announce that two research articles have been accepted for publication in *Fuel* and the *International Journal of Hydrogen Energy*.

## 1. *Fuel*

**Title:** *Oxygenated Aromatic Blending of Methyl Benzoate into TPRF Surrogates: Sooting Propensity, Soot Reactivity, and Nanostructural Characterization for Advanced Gasoline Formulations*

This study is the outcome of an international research collaboration between the **Indian Institute of Technology Delhi (IIT Delhi)**, India, and **Sophia University**, Japan, under the **LOTUS Programme**, supported by the Japan Science and Technology Agency (JST).

The research investigates the effects of methyl benzoate blending on soot formation, soot oxidation reactivity, and soot nanostructure, contributing to the development of advanced gasoline formulations aimed at reducing particulate emissions.

---

## 2. *International Journal of Hydrogen Energy*

**Title:** *Effect of Nozzle Geometry on the Combustion Characteristics of Ammonia/Oxygen in a Constant-Volume Combustion Chamber with a Pre-Chamber Configuration*

This publication builds upon research presented at the **10th International Hydrogen Technologies Congress (IHTEC-2026)**, held on May 10-13, 2026, in Istanbul, Türkiye.

The study examines the influence of pre-chamber nozzle size and configuration on ammonia/oxygen combustion characteristics in a constant-volume combustion chamber, contributing to the advancement of ammonia-based combustion technologies.
