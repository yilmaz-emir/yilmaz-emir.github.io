Researcher profile has been published on the website of the Zero Emission Mobility Power Source Research Consortium (ZEM Consortium), coordinated by the National Institute of Advanced Industrial Science and Technology (AIST), Japan.
The profile introduces my research activities in tribology, surface engineering, and ammonia-fueled internal combustion engines, with an emphasis on improving the efficiency and durability of mechanical systems.
The consortium provides opportunities for research collaboration and knowledge exchange between academia and industry.

Visit the <a href="https://unit.aist.go.jp/rieet/egsrg/zemconso/memberprofile.html">ZEM Consortium Researcher Profiles</a> to learn more.
