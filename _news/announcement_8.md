Excited to share that our collaborative <a href="[https://www.sophia.ac.jp/eng/article/news/topics/251024_ap/](https://www.jst.go.jp/program/india/project/2025.html)">application</a> to the JST–India LOTUS Programme has been accepted. 
Under the leadership of <a href="[https://www.sophia.ac.jp/eng/article/news/topics/251024_ap/"(https://www.jst.go.jp/program/india/project/2025.html)">Professor Ichiyanagi</a> and <a href="[[https://www.sophia.ac.jp/eng/article/news/topics/251024_ap/"(https://www.jst.go.jp/program/india/project/2025.html)](https://chemical.iitd.ac.in/faculty/abhijeet-raj/)">Professor Raj</a>, this project will integrate experimental and numerical studies to investigate how oxygen-containing and carbon-free additives affect the formation of polycyclic aromatic hydrocarbons (PAHs), soot, and NOx during the combustion of conventional fuels, contributing to cleaner combustion strategies.
